Growing up.

What happens when the pressure to be better becomes the feeling that you are never enough?

Through everyday images, personal reflections and interviews, Twenty explores the invisible pressure of productivity culture, digital validation and comparison in early adulthood.

WHAT TO LOOK FOR

A title becomes an entry point.

The early assembly shows the handwritten notebook. In the final opening, the red Twenty title is layered over that same everyday surface. The personal question becomes the film’s introduction.

Notice how the title treatment changes the role of the notebook: from an observed object to the beginning of a story.
