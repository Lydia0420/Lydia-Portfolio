# Local texture sources

All downloaded from Poly Haven, CC0 asset library. Original channel files are preserved; adjustments are shader based.

- Tree Bark 03: https://polyhaven.com/a/tree_bark_03
- Beige Wall 001: https://polyhaven.com/a/beige_wall_001
- Marble 01: https://polyhaven.com/a/marble_01
- Oak Veneer 01: https://polyhaven.com/a/oak_veneer_01
- License: https://polyhaven.com/license

Each set includes 1K diffuse, displacement and roughness. URLs and original MD5 checksums are recorded in textures/manifest.json. All twelve files matched their supplied checksums. Floor colour variation is intentionally reduced for the approved pale stone palette. Displacement is used as a micro-normal signal, not full geometry displacement.

Doorframes use a cropped stone sample to avoid tile seams across the arch. Gold and ceramic use subtle shader microtexture; they are not newly scanned metal or ceramic assets.
