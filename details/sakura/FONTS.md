# Falls Chinese typography

`falls-serif.woff2` is a Noto Serif SC Regular subset of the text in
`sakura.html`. Source: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk/tree/main/Serif
License: SIL OFL 1.1, included at `../shared/fonts/graphic-serif-OFL.txt`.
New Chinese copy may require rebuilding the subset. System fallbacks are
provided. The English hero title continues to use the original Aldrich face.
